Where everything actually is

1 · The whole trip

Numbered pins = the 7 nights, in order. Coloured lines = each travel leg — click any of them for the times.

2 · Bled & Bohinj — the bit that confuses everyone

The two-stations trap: Lesce-Bled (red) is on the main line and is 4 km from the lake — most trains stop there and it is NOT yours. You arrive at Bled Jezero, the little lakeside halt (green). · Zasip is a hill village 4 km north — downhill to the lake, uphill coming home, so don't plan to walk it. · Lake Bohinj is a different lake, 26 km southwest. Saturday is a loop: bus to Ukanc (west end) → cable car up → boat back east to Ribčev Laz → bus home.

3 · Trieste

Your flat is up on the San Giusto hill — the steep bit. Grotta Gigante is up on the Karst plateau (bus 42); Miramare is up the coast (bus 6/36); the airport is 33 km northwest and not in Trieste.

❌ What got dropped, and why

Vintgar Gorge. It's the reason the Zasip farmhouse was booked ("walkable from the door") — but the gorge has changed. The boardwalk is one-way only: 1.6 km in, and you cannot walk back through it.
